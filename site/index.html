PC hardware monitor for Android

Monitor your PC from your phone.

SysWatch is a PC monitor for your phone. A small Windows app reads your CPU and GPU load and temperature, memory, network, storage and battery. Your Android phone shows them live, like a system monitor you can carry around, and tells you when one crosses a limit you set.

Your PC stats on your phone, as they move

Cards for CPU, GPU, memory, network, storage, battery and uptime, each with its own history graph. SysWatch only shows real readings: if your PC does not report something, it is left out.

Know when your PC runs hot

Set a limit on any gauge. Your phone tells you when it is crossed, so you can watch CPU and GPU temperature on your phone without staring at it.

CPU load above 90%

Battery level below 20%

Do not repeat for 5 minutes
  • Rules read as sentences, such as “CPU load above 90%” or “Battery level below 20%”.
  • Each limit is drawn as a red line on its gauge, bar and graph.
  • Limits on load, memory, battery, network speed, storage and temperatures.
  • A quiet period per alert, from 1 minute to 1 hour, so one spike is one notification.
  • One-tap starter alerts, and export and import as a file.

Make it yours

Pick an accent and a theme, then put the cards in your order. It stays one calm panel either way.

Your accent colour

Amber, cyan, green, violet, rose or mono, or matched to your wallpaper on Android 12 and newer.

Six styles, three themes

Stickers, Solid, Banner, Tiles, Pills or Classic, each in light, dark or true black.

Five Overview layouts

Bridge, Gauges, Strip charts, Ledger and Focus.

Your cards, your order

Choose which cards show and in what order, roomy or compact.

A second screen for your desk

Stand the phone next to your keyboard and it becomes a second screen for your PC stats: 29 always-on standby faces, 16 instruments, 8 art faces and 5 music faces, in portrait and landscape.

  • 8 art faces: Horizon, Blocks, Bauhaus, Ink, Weave, Bloom, Type and Pixel. Ink sets your readings like a printed page; Bauhaus turns CPU, GPU and memory into shapes.
  • 16 instrument faces, picked from a gallery that previews each one with your own readings.
  • Burn-in protection. Faces can dim when idle or stay bright, framed or filling the whole screen.
  • A home screen widget in four looks: rings, bars, numerals, trace.

See what your PC is playing

Music or a video on the PC shows on your phone with its cover: on Overview, and in five music standby faces for the desk.

  • A now playing card on Overview, with the title, artist and cover.
  • Five music standby faces: Poster, Ambient, Glossy, Vinyl and Quiet.
  • Play, pause and skip from the phone. It is on by default; switch it off in Settings on the PC.

Local and Tailscale mode only. What you play never goes through the cloud.

A small app on your PC

The Windows app reads the hardware and stays out of the way in the system tray.

Light in the tray

Hidden to the tray it uses about 55 to 73 MB of memory and under 0.5% of one CPU core. Lightweight mode takes a reading every 3 seconds and sends every 5 seconds in Cloud mode.

In your language

English, German, French, Spanish or Russian. It starts in your Windows language, and you can change it in setup or in Settings.

Easy on the cloud

In Cloud mode the PC sends at most one update every 2 seconds.

Set up in three steps

No account and nothing to type: the QR code carries everything the phone needs.

  1. 1

    Install on your PC

    Download the Windows installer and run it. The installer is not code signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows a warning: choose More info, then Run anyway. SysWatch then sits in the system tray.

  2. 2

    Install on your phone

    Get SysWatch on Google Play and open it.

  3. 3

    Scan the code

    Open Pairing on the PC and scan the QR code with the phone.

Privacy, plainly

No account

A 6-character pairing code is the only thing linking phone and PC.

Read only, with one exception

The phone can see readings. It cannot change or run anything on your PC, except play, pause and skip for media in Local and Tailscale mode, when you allow it on your PC.

Local stays local

Local mode keeps readings on your own network. It is not encrypted there, so use a network you trust, or Tailscale, which encrypts the link.

Cloud, said straight

Cloud by DevLune passes readings, and your phone’s name, through DevLune’s relay so the phone can reach the PC from anywhere.

Nothing extra

Running program names are not sent unless you switch that on. The camera is used only to scan the pairing QR code. What is playing is never sent through the cloud.

This website

No cookies, no trackers, and nothing loaded from other sites.

Questions people ask

Is SysWatch free?

Yes. SysWatch is free on the phone and on the PC, with no ads and no account. If it earns a place on your desk, you can sponsor SysWatch on GitHub.

Does it work away from home?

Yes, in two of the three connection modes. Cloud by DevLune works from anywhere with internet and needs no setup. Tailscale is instant and private from anywhere, after about five minutes of setup with the Tailscale guide. Local works on the same Wi-Fi.

Does it need an account?

No. A 6-character pairing code is the only thing linking phone and PC. You scan one QR code and there is nothing to type.

What can the phone do to my PC?

Very little, on purpose. SysWatch is read only, except play, pause and skip for media in Local and Tailscale mode, when you allow it on your PC. That control is on by default and you switch it off in Settings on the PC. The phone cannot change or run anything else.

Can the phone show what my PC is playing?

Yes, in Local and Tailscale mode. The phone shows the title, artist and cover on Overview and in five music standby faces, and can play, pause and skip. None of it goes through the cloud.

Which PCs are supported?

SysWatch runs on Windows today: Windows 10 and Windows 11, 64-bit. The phone app needs Android 7.0 or newer.

Why does Windows warn about the installer?

The installer is not code signed yet, so Windows SmartScreen shows “Windows protected your PC”. Choose More info, then Run anyway. The SysWatch install guide walks through every step.

Does it show CPU temperature?

Yes, when your PC reports it. SysWatch shows CPU and GPU temperature next to load, and NVIDIA graphics cards are read directly. CPU temperature depends on what your PC reports, and SysWatch never shows a reading your PC does not send.

Which languages is it in?

Both apps are in English, German, French, Spanish and Russian. The Windows app starts in your Windows language, and you can change it in setup or in Settings.

How do I add my language?

Community translations are welcome, and no programming is needed: it is one text file per language. In the translations folder of the SysWatch repository on GitHub, copy en.xml, translate it and open a pull request. A merged translation reaches the app by itself, with no app update. The translation guide walks through each step and the five rules.

Is my data sent anywhere?

That depends on the connection you pick. In Local mode readings never leave your network. Over Tailscale they travel through your own private, encrypted link. With Cloud by DevLune, readings and your phone’s name pass through DevLune’s relay. Running program names are not sent unless you switch that on. What is playing on your PC is never sent through the cloud.

Help translate SysWatch

Both apps, on the phone and on Windows, ship in English, German, French, Spanish and Russian. Community translations are welcome: add your language, or fix a line that reads wrong in yours. A merged translation reaches the apps by itself, with no app update.
