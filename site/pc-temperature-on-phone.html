PC temperature guide

Check your PC’s CPU and GPU temperature from your phone

A game, a render or a long export heats a PC up, and the screen you would check is the one that is busy. SysWatch puts your PC’s CPU and GPU temperature on your Android phone, live, and tells you when one crosses a limit you set.

What you need

  • A PC with Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit.
  • A phone with Android 7.0 or newer.
  • The same Wi-Fi for both to begin with. Cloud and Tailscale work from anywhere.

Set it up

  1. Install SysWatch on the PC. The install guide has the download and walks you past the Windows warning.

  2. Install SysWatch on your phone from Google Play.

  3. Open SysWatch on the PC, go to Pairing and scan the QR code with the phone app.

  4. Open Overview on the phone. CPU and GPU temperature sit beside load, each with a graph of the last minutes.

Get told when it runs hot

You do not have to watch it. Open Alerts, pick CPU temperature or GPU temperature and set a limit, for example above 85°C. The limit is drawn as a red line on the gauge, and your phone notifies you when it is crossed.

  • A quiet period per alert, from 1 minute to 1 hour, so one spike is one notification.
  • Alerts also work on load, memory, battery, network speed and storage.
  • Starter alerts set the common ones in one tap.

Keep it in view

For a long session, stand the phone by the keyboard and open standby: an always-on screen that shows temperature and load in large type. See how to use a phone as a PC stats display.

If no temperature shows

SysWatch shows the temperatures your PC reports and never invents one. NVIDIA graphics cards are read directly. CPU temperature depends on the PC: on some machines Windows does not expose it, and the reading stays empty instead of showing a guess.

Away from home

Local mode works on the same Wi-Fi. To check temperatures from anywhere else, choose Cloud by DevLune, which needs no setup, or Tailscale, which keeps the link private and instant.
